EV FAQs

1. What is an electric vehicle (EV)?
An EV is a vehicle that runs fully or partially on electric power instead of gasoline.
2. How long does it take to charge an EV?
Charging time varies between 30 minutes to 8 hours depending on the charger type and battery size.
3. How much does it cost to charge an EV?
It usually costs between €5 and €15 depending on battery size and charging station.
4. What are the common connector types?
Common connectors include CCS, CHAdeMO, and Type 2 for AC charging.
5. Can I charge my EV at home?
Yes, using a standard outlet or a faster home charger.
6. Are there government grants for EVs?
Yes, in Ireland there are SEAI grants and tax incentives for EV purchases and home chargers.
7. How far can an EV travel on a full charge?
It ranges from 150 km to over 500 km depending on the EV model.
8. Can I use Google Maps for EV stations?
Yes, Google Maps shows nearby charging stations with live availability for many networks.
9. What is regenerative braking?
It's a system that recovers energy when braking and sends it back to the battery.
10. Do EVs require less maintenance?
Yes. EVs have fewer moving parts and no oil changes, so they require less maintenance overall.
